About the Cause

Meet the Golden Lion Tamarin 🌟

The Golden Lion Tamarin is a tiny, gorgeous monkey with bright golden fur — kind of like a little lion! They live in the Atlantic Coastal rainforests of Brazil, and they are one of the most endangered primates on Earth.

Humans have cut down over 93% of their forest home to build farms and cities, leaving fewer than 2,500 Golden Lion Tamarins in the wild today. They are listed as Endangered on the IUCN Red List, which means they could disappear from our planet forever without help.

Conservation programs work hard to protect the remaining forest, breed Tamarins in zoos, and release them back into the wild. Every donation helps fund these efforts — and even a small amount can make a big difference! 🌱

🦁 Under 2,500 left in the wild
🌳 93% of habitat lost
🇧🇷 Found only in Brazil
⚖️ Weighs less than a pound
🔴 IUCN: Endangered
