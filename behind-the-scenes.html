The Prompt That Started It All

Gabriel's Original Prompt
I need to create a single web page for taking in donations. The cause for the donations is in support of the Golden Lion Tamarin, because they are an endangered species.

I am a first-grade student at Birdrock Elementary in San Diego, and this is part of a monthly community outreach program.

The page should have a video that I will record at the top. We will have multiple options for a donation, namely Venmo and GoFundMe. Please add a note that my parents will match donations up to $1000.

The web page should expand on why the Tamarin is endangered, in a few sentences along with a picture or two.

The page should also give few details about my school, especially about my teacher Ms. Nelson, who is awesome and leads the community outreach program.

The donation section should be at the end, and we should have a bold text showing the total amount raised up to date.

This is the seed of everything. A 1st grader's words set a whole webpage, two videos, and a conservation mission in motion. That's the power of asking — and then doing the work.

A Message for Every Kid Who Wants to Try

"

Hard Work Hides Behind Every Great Story

When you watch Gabriel's videos on his fundraiser page, they look smooth, confident, and easy. But this page tells the truth: nothing great starts perfect. There were attempts that got cut off, moments where he had to start over, and takes where the words just didn't come out right. And every single one of those moments made the final version better.

Gabriel is a first grader who cares deeply about animals and the planet. He didn't wait until he was older. He didn't wait until it was easy. He picked a cause, asked for help, and then did the work — again and again — until he got it right.

The world only sees the finished page, the polished video, the fundraiser total climbing. But behind every successful story is a kid (or a person of any age) who tried, failed quietly, and tried again. That invisible effort is the most important part.

So if you have an idea — a cause you care about, an animal you want to protect, a difference you want to make — start. Make the messy first attempt. Record the blooper. Write the rough draft. Because the only way to have a finished story is to begin an unfinished one.

🦁 Be part of this world — not just in it. 🌿
